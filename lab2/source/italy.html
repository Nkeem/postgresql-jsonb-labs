Top 97 Italy Startups to Watch in 2026

Startups

Italy's startup landscape features a blend of established industries and modern technology sectors. Companies span from fashion and luxury goods to software and artificial intelligence.

Mobile app developer Bending Spoons leads with $4.2 billion in funding. Meanwhile, newer ventures like AI legal assistant Lexroom.ai are also securing significant capital.

Below is our list of 97 Italy startups worth keeping an eye on.

Apps Mobile Apps Software Video Editing

Bending Spoons builds and manages a suite of widely-used mobile applications. The company's technology powers popular creative tools in categories like video editing and AI-powered photo enhancement.
