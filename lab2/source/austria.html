Top 76 Austria Startups to Watch in 2026

Startups

Austria's startup ecosystem is diverse, with companies specializing in AI, fintech, and travel. The region supports both newly formed ventures and more established businesses.

Major players include the wellness company Therme Group, which has raised $565M. At the same time, new AI companies like mypaperwork.ai are just starting out with pre-seed funding.

Below is our list of 76 Austria startups worth keeping an eye on.
