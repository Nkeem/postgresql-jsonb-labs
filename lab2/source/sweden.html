Top 100 Sweden Startups to Watch in 2026

Startups

Sweden's technology landscape shows a strong concentration in artificial intelligence, with companies applying it across diverse sectors. The ecosystem features both recently founded startups and large, established software enterprises.

Recent examples include AI developer platform Lovable, which raised $222.5M since its 2023 founding. In the mobility sector, freight tech company Einride has attracted $752.3M in total funding.

Below is our list of 100 Sweden startups worth keeping an eye on.

AI Developer Platform Developer Tools Software

Lovable provides an AI-powered platform for building software applications without needing extensive coding skills. Their tool uses artificial intelligence to automate complex development, allowing teams to ship products faster.
