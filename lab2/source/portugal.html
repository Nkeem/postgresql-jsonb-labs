Top 100 Portugal Startups to Watch in 2026

Startups

Portugal's startup ecosystem features a diverse range of companies, from new ventures to established industry names. These businesses operate in key sectors including aerospace, finance, and artificial intelligence.

Leading the pack in funding is aerospace firm TEKEVER, which has raised over $630 million. Other major players include the AI fraud detection company Feedzai, with more than $352 million in total funding.

Below is our list of 100 Portugal startups worth keeping an eye on.

Failory's Top 10 Picks

1. Feedzai

Analytics AI FinTech Fraud Detection

Feedzai develops an AI-powered platform that gives banks and fintechs a single, comprehensive view of financial risk. The technology works in real-time to detect and prevent fraud and money laundering, securing transactions for both businesses and their customers.

Blockchain Cryptocurrency DeFi Ethereum Finance FinTech

Ethena developed a synthetic dollar protocol on Ethereum that provides a crypto-native alternative to traditional financial systems. Their core product, USDe, is a scalable form of digital money designed to operate independently from legacy banking infrastructure.

xMoney offers a payment platform that enables businesses to securely accept and process cryptocurrency. The platform is designed to build consumer trust by providing both secure transactions and purchase protection.
