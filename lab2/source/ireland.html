Top 99 Ireland Startups to Watch in 2026

Startups

Ireland's startup ecosystem shows strength across several key sectors, including financial technology and enterprise software. The majority of these leading companies are based in Dublin.

E-commerce funding provider Wayflyer has attracted $1.9 billion in capital. Meanwhile, cybersecurity firm Tines has raised $271.2 million for its automation platform.

Below is our list of 99 Ireland startups worth keeping an eye on.

Failory's Top 9 Picks

1. Tines

Cyber Security Data Integration Enterprise Software Industrial Automation Information Services Security Software

Tines builds a no-code automation platform designed to help security teams streamline their most critical workflows. Their software allows professionals to connect disparate tools and automate manual processes without writing a single line of code.
