Top 100 Singapore Startups to Watch in 2026

Startups

Singapore's startup environment is a major hub for financial technology and artificial intelligence companies. The city-state hosts a variety of businesses from early stage to large organizations.

Companies like Airwallex and MiniMax have each secured $1.2 billion in funding. Others, such as insurtech firm bolttech, have also raised significant capital with over $690 million.

Below is our list of 100 Singapore startups worth keeping an eye on.

Bitget runs a crypto derivatives exchange platform that delivers comprehensive and secure trading solutions. Their exchange supports a wide array of digital assets and advanced features for traders around the world.
