Top 100 Finland Startups to Watch in 2026

Startups

Finland's startup landscape is defined by highly funded companies in specialized sectors. For example, RELEX Solutions has attracted over $814 million for its supply chain platform.

The country is also a base for leaders in hardware and quantum computing. Oura has raised $598.3 million for its wellness rings, while IQM Quantum Computers is developing next generation systems.

Below is our list of 100 Finland startups worth keeping an eye on.

Failory's Top 10 Picks

B2C Health Care IoT Mobile Apps Wearables

Oura designs and develops a smart ring that tracks sleep, activity, and other key health metrics. The wearable provides personalized insights to help users understand their body's readiness and improve their overall well-being.
