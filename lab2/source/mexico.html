Top 100 Mexico Startups to Watch in 2026

Startups

Mexico's startup ecosystem is defined by major investments in its fintech and energy industries. Mexico City stands out as the primary hub for these emerging companies.

Fintechs like Kapital and Plata have secured substantial funding, with Kapital raising $471.1 million. The cleantech sector also shows strength through companies like VEMO, which has gathered $373.3 million.

Below is our list of 100 Mexico startups worth keeping an eye on.

Financial Services FinTech Productivity Tools Small and Medium Businesses Wealth Management

Kapital developed an all-in-one platform for businesses in Latin America to manage their finances and company spending. They provide corporate credit cards and financing tools that give teams better control over their expenses.

BabyQuip built a marketplace for renting baby equipment, making it easier for families to travel without packing bulky gear. Their platform connects parents with local providers who deliver items like cribs and strollers, and they also offer a professional cleaning service for baby gear.
