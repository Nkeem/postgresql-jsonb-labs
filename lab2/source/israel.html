Top 100 Israel Startups to Watch in 2026

Startups

Israel's startup scene is defined by its deep technology focus, particularly in artificial intelligence and cybersecurity. The following companies represent 100 of the country's most significant ventures.

Among them is Aidoc, which has raised $414M to apply AI in healthcare. Another company, Fetcherr, is using AI to change airline pricing with $156.5M in funding.

Below is our list of 100 Israel startups worth keeping an eye on.
