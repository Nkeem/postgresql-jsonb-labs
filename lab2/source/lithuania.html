Top 58 Lithuania Startups to Watch in 2026

Startups

Lithuania's startup ecosystem shows a strong concentration in financial technology and artificial intelligence. The landscape features both newly formed ventures and established firms attracting significant investment.

For instance, the recently founded Vorpen AI has secured €300M in funding. Other major players include real estate company Akropolis with $350M and cybersecurity firm Nord Security with $209M.

Below is our list of 58 Lithuania startups worth keeping an eye on.
