Top 42 Argentina Startups to Watch in 2026

Startups

Argentina's startup scene shows a strong focus on financial technology. New companies are also emerging in important fields like agriculture and biotechnology.

Fintech company Ualá leads the group with $910M in funding. Other businesses like Puna Bio are also growing in the country's AgTech sector.

Below is our list of 42 Argentina startups worth keeping an eye on.

Failory's Top 9 Picks

1. Remitee

Financial Services FinTech Payments

Remitee developed a digital platform for multi-purpose cross-border payments and financial services. Their technology simplifies how people send and receive money internationally, streamlining the remittance process.

HeadquartersBuenos Aires, Distrito Federal, Argentina
Year Founded2016
FoundersEduardo Hamel, Sergio Saravia
Funding Amount$20M
Startup SizeLean Team (11-50)
Last Funding StatusVenture Round

2. Ualá

Banking Credit Cards Financial Services FinTech Payments

Ualá built a financial ecosystem that gives users in Latin America a digital banking app and a prepaid card. Their platform simplifies personal finance by centralizing services like payments, transfers, and investments into a single mobile experience.

HeadquartersBuenos Aires, Distrito Federal, Argentina
Year Founded2017
FoundersPierpaolo Barbieri
Funding Amount$910M
Startup SizeMajor Organization (1,001-5,000)
Last Funding StatusSeries E
Top InvestorsGeneral Catalyst, Bessemer Venture Partners, Endeavor Catalyst, Goldman Sachs, Ribbit Capital

3. Pomelo

Credit Cards Financial Services FinTech

Pomelo builds the financial technology infrastructure for companies to launch and manage their own card programs. Their platform allows businesses across Latin America to issue, process, and manage credit, debit, and prepaid cards.

HeadquartersBuenos Aires, Distrito Federal, Argentina

4. ALKEMIO

CleanTech Manufacturing Mining Mining Technology

ALKEMIO develops modular refining technology designed to create local supply chains for rare earth elements. Their approach provides a more sustainable and efficient way to process these critical minerals, helping to decentralize a traditionally complex global supply chain.

FinTech Payments

Tapi builds fintech infrastructure that simplifies payments for businesses across Latin America. Their platform offers e-wallet solutions and merchant services, enabling seamless transactions both online and in-person.

Biotechnology Food Processing

BioBlends creates gas-based biopreservatives that naturally extend the shelf life of fresh foods. Their solution helps reduce food waste and improve safety across the supply chain.

7. Puna Bio

Agriculture AgTech Biotechnology

Puna Bio develops biological inputs for agriculture by unlocking the potential of extremophiles, microorganisms adapted to the world's most extreme environments. Their technology helps increase crop yields and restore degraded soil while reducing the overall carbon footprint of farming.

AI E-Learning EdTech Education

Egg builds an AI-powered educational platform that teaches coding through a unique human cooperation model. The company's technology forms optimal learning teams and provides personalized paths for students to master in-demand tech skills.

Addiction Treatment Big Data Child Care Elder Care Health Care Home Health Care Mental Health
