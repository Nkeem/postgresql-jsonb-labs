Top 100 Norway Startups to Watch in 2026

Startups

Norway's startup ecosystem is defined by its focus on industrial and enterprise technology. Companies are building specialized hardware and software for global markets.

This includes AutoStore, an automated storage company with $2.8B in funding, and 1X, which is developing humanoid robots. The full list showcases a range of companies from AgTech to AI.

Below is our list of 100 Norway startups worth keeping an eye on.

Failory's Top 10 Picks

1. 1X

AI Manufacturing Robotics

The engineering team at 1X builds humanoid robots designed to work safely alongside people in commercial environments. These androids are created to augment the global labor force, capable of performing tasks with human-like movements.

HeadquartersMoss, Ostfold, Norway
Year Founded2014
FoundersBernt Øivind Børnich, Phuong Nguyen
Funding Amount$136.5M
Startup SizeMid-Size Team (51-250)
Last Funding StatusSeries B

2. reMarkable

Consumer Electronics Digital Media Mobile Devices Software

reMarkable has created a digital tablet for those who prefer the tactile experience of writing on paper. Their device is designed for focused work, replacing notebooks and printed documents with a paper-like digital display.

HeadquartersOslo, Oslo, Norway
Year Founded2013

3. Cognite

AI Industrial Automation ML SaaS

Cognite develops an industrial DataOps platform that enables the digital transformation of heavy-asset industries like manufacturing and energy. Their core software, Cognite Data Fusion®, contextualizes operational data to make it accessible and useful for AI applications and large-scale analytics.

HeadquartersLysaker, Akershus, Norway
Year Founded2016

4. Nofence

Agriculture AgTech IoT

Nofence created the world's first virtual fencing system for grazing animals. Their technology uses solar-powered GPS collars to contain livestock within designated pastures, removing the need for physical fences.

Advertising Apps Classifieds Mobile Advertising Sustainability

AI Customer Service ML Software Virtual Assistant

Boost AI develops a conversational AI platform that allows enterprises to build and deploy advanced virtual assistants. Their technology is used to automate customer service and internal support, resolving complex inquiries at scale.

7. AutoStore

Automotive Industrial Automation Warehouse Automation

AutoStore develops a high-density automated storage and retrieval system that uses robots to manage inventory within a cubic grid. This system allows businesses to significantly increase their storage capacity and automate order fulfillment within their existing warehouse space.

Analytics B2B Cloud Management Compliance Enterprise Software Innovation Management IT Management Risk Management SaaS Software

Ardoq builds a data-driven platform for Enterprise Architecture, helping organizations manage complex digital transformations. Their tool creates a dynamic, visual map of the business that empowers leaders to make faster and smarter decisions.

Analytics Blockchain Cryptocurrency Ethereum Trading Platform

Dune Analytics built a community-powered platform that makes on-chain crypto data accessible through custom queries and dashboards. The tool is widely used by analysts and investors to explore, extract, and visualize complex data from blockchains like Ethereum.

10. Saga Robotics

Agriculture AgTech Robotics

Saga Robotics develops autonomous robots to help farmers manage a wide variety of agricultural operations. Their flagship robot, Thorvald, is known for its ability to treat plant diseases with UV light, helping growers reduce their use of chemical pesticides.
