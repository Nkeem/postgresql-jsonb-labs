Top 76 Colombia Startups to Watch in 2026

Startups

Colombia's startup scene is growing, with a strong focus on the fintech and retail sectors. Many of the country's top companies are concentrated in its capital, Bogotá.

On-demand delivery company Rappi leads the pack with over $2.6 billion in funding. Other major players like fintech company Addi and real estate platform Habi have also secured hundreds of millions.

Below is our list of 76 Colombia startups worth keeping an eye on.

Failory's Top 10 Picks

1. Rappi

Consumer Goods Delivery E-Commerce Food and Beverage Food Delivery Mobile Apps

Rappi built a "super-app" for on-demand delivery, bringing everything from restaurant meals and groceries to pharmacy items directly to customers' doors. The platform connects a network of couriers with local businesses, creating a digital marketplace for fast, urban commerce.

HeadquartersBogotá, Distrito Especial, Colombia
Year Founded2015

Funding Amount$2.6B

Consumer Goods E-Commerce Pet Retail

LAIKA operates an e-commerce platform for pets that offers a wide selection of products and services. They aim to be a one-stop shop for pet owners, covering everything from food and supplies to veterinary care.
