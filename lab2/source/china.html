Top 100 China Startups to Watch in 2026

Startups

China's startup scene is heavily focused on artificial intelligence and advanced manufacturing. Many of the country's top companies are building foundational AI models and next-generation robotics.

For example, Moonshot AI has secured $1.6 billion in funding since its founding in 2023. Below is a broader look at the companies shaping China's technology landscape.

Below is our list of 100 China startups worth keeping an eye on.
