Top 100 Japan Startups to Watch in 2026

Startups

Japan's startup environment is defined by its focus on deep technology and mobility. Companies are developing solutions in fields from autonomous vehicles to quantum computing.

Automotive software firm Tier IV has raised $457.8M to develop its technology. Newer entrants like Sakana AI, founded in 2023, have also attracted large investments for generative AI.

Below is our list of 100 Japan startups worth keeping an eye on.
