Top 98 Denmark Startups to Watch in 2026

Startups

Denmark's startup ecosystem is defined by its strength in financial technology, AI, and enterprise software. The majority of its leading companies are concentrated in the capital city of Copenhagen.

This environment has produced highly funded companies like Pleo, which has raised over $470 million. It also supports newer ventures in fields like AI healthcare and cybersecurity.

Below is our list of 98 Denmark startups worth keeping an eye on.

Failory's Top 9 Picks

1. Pleo

Financial Services FinTech Mobile Payments Payments

Pleo issues smart company cards that allow employees to easily pay for business expenses. Their platform automates expense reports and gives finance teams real-time control over all company spending.

HeadquartersCopenhagen, Hovedstaden, Denmark
Year Founded2015

Funding Amount$470.9M

2. Keepit

Cloud Data Services Cyber Security Enterprise Software SaaS

Keepit builds a data protection platform for backing up and recovering information from popular SaaS applications like Microsoft 365 and Salesforce. Their service secures this data in a vendor-independent cloud, ensuring it remains protected and separate from the original provider.

AI B2B Customer Service Software

AI Health Care Software

Accounting Database Financial Services

Light has built a financial platform that provides multinational companies with a fully automated general ledger. The platform is designed to handle complex accounting needs, streamlining financial operations for businesses that operate across different countries.
