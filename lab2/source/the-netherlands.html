Top 100 Startups from The Netherlands to Watch in 2026

Startups

The Netherlands' startup ecosystem is defined by its diversity and ability to secure significant funding. The country is home to growing companies across sectors like finance, AI, and healthcare.

Fintech company Mambu leads with €387M in funding, while neobank bunq has also raised a substantial €366.9M. These companies represent the top tier of the nation's emerging businesses.

Below is our list of 100 startups from The Netherlands worth keeping an eye on.

Framer provides a visual canvas for designing and building professional websites without writing any code. Designers favor the tool for its ability to turn complex prototypes into fully responsive, production-ready websites.
