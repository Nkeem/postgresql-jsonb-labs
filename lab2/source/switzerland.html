Top 98 Switzerland Startups to Watch in 2026

Startups

Switzerland's startup landscape features a strong focus on deep tech and financial technology. The nation hosts a variety of companies working on everything from robotics to environmental engineering.

This environment has produced heavily funded companies, including Climeworks with $946.1M for carbon capture. Cybersecurity firm Acronis is another major player, having secured $658M in funding.

Below is our list of 98 Switzerland startups worth keeping an eye on.

Failory's Top 8 Picks

1. Auterion

Drone Management Drones Open Source Operating Systems Robotics Software

Auterion develops a common software platform that powers drones, robots, and other autonomous vehicles. Their open-source operating system allows enterprise and government users to manage entire fleets of different robots through a single, unified interface.

HeadquartersZürich, Zurich, Switzerland
Year Founded2017
FoundersKevin Sartori, Lorenz Meier
Funding Amount$181M
Startup SizeMid-Size Team (51-250)
Last Funding StatusSeries B
Top InvestorsBessemer Venture Partners, Lakestar, Costanoa Ventures, U.S. Department of Defense, Mosaic Ventures

2. Corintis

Electronics Industrial Engineering Product Research Semiconductor

Corintis develops microfluidic cooling solutions that integrate directly with data center chips, boosting their performance and energy efficiency. Their technology addresses the critical thermal challenges of high-performance computing, enabling more powerful and sustainable operations.

HeadquartersLausanne, Vaud, Switzerland

3. Acronis

AI Cloud Data Services Cloud Security Cyber Security Software

Acronis unifies data protection and cybersecurity into a single, automated platform for businesses and individuals. Their solutions offer integrated backup, disaster recovery, and AI-powered security to defend against modern digital threats.

4. ANYbotics

Industrial Automation Machinery Manufacturing Manufacturing RPA Robotics

ANYbotics creates autonomous legged robots designed to handle inspection and monitoring in complex industrial environments. Their flagship robot, ANYmal, navigates challenging terrain to provide operators with crucial data and improve worksite safety.

Battery Energy Energy Storage Renewable Energy

Unbound Potential develops and provides large-scale energy storage systems for a variety of applications. Their battery technology is designed to support the broader transition to renewable energy sources.

6. Teylor

Banking Financial Services FinTech Software

Teylor built a digital lending platform that provides fast and convenient loans to small and medium-sized businesses. By streamlining the entire credit process, they help companies secure financing much more quickly than through traditional banking.

7. Climeworks

Carbon Capture CleanTech Environmental Engineering GreenTech

Climeworks builds and operates direct air capture facilities designed to remove CO₂ directly from the atmosphere. They offer this carbon dioxide removal as a service, enabling businesses and individuals to neutralize their emissions permanently.

HeadquartersZürich, Zurich, Switzerland

8. Cutiss

Beauty Biotechnology Cosmetics Health Care

Cutiss is a life science company that engineers personalized skin grafts using regenerative medicine and tissue engineering. Their therapies are designed to treat patients with large skin defects, offering a new solution for skin regeneration.
