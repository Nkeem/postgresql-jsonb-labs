Top 100 Germany Startups to Watch in 2026

Startups

Germany's startup ecosystem shows a clear focus on deep tech and artificial intelligence. Many of its leading companies are building foundational technologies in these fields.

For example, AI defense company Helsing has raised €1.4B, while translation service DeepL has secured $400M in funding. These companies represent the top tier of Germany's growing list of successful ventures.

Below is our list of 100 Germany startups worth keeping an eye on.
