Top 100 United Kingdom Startups to Watch in 2026

Startups

The United Kingdom's startup landscape shows strong activity in the AI and Fintech industries. These two sectors are home to many of the nation's most well funded new companies.

Newcomers like AI platform Nscale have raised significant capital, securing $1.7B in its founding year. Established players such as Checkout.com and Monzo also feature prominently with total funding of $1.8B each.

Below is our list of 100 United Kingdom startups worth keeping an eye on.
