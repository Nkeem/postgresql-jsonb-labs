Top 100 Estonia Startups to Watch in 2026

Startups

Estonia's startup scene is defined by its diversity, with companies making strides in sectors from AI to green energy. This environment supports both large, established firms and new ventures.

Mobility company Bolt stands out with $2.3 billion in funding. Following behind are companies like Veriff, which focuses on identity verification, and Stargate Hydrogen in the renewable energy space.

Below is our list of 100 Estonia startups worth keeping an eye on.

Failory's Top 10 Picks

1. Bolt

Car Sharing Food Delivery Mobile Apps Ride Sharing Transportation

Bolt built an all-in-one mobility app that provides ride-hailing, food and grocery delivery, and shared car and scooter services. They offer a more affordable and convenient alternative to traditional transportation and delivery options in cities around the world.
