Top 100 France Startups to Watch in 2026

Startups

France's startup ecosystem shows strength across many sectors, from artificial intelligence to biotechnology and sustainable technology. The country is home to both new, fast-growing companies and more established market leaders.

For instance, AI company Mistral AI has secured €2.8B in funding since its founding in 2023. Another newcomer, Bioptimus, raised $76M in its first year to develop AI for biology.

Below is our list of 100 France startups worth keeping an eye on.

AI Generative AI ML NLP Open Source

Mistral AI creates powerful large language models, offering both open-source and commercial versions for enterprise use. The company’s platform provides a suite of AI services and APIs tailored to diverse business applications.
