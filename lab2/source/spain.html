Top 100 Spain Startups to Watch in 2026

Startups

Spain's startup ecosystem features a diverse range of industries, from real estate to renewable energy. The country is home to both well-established companies and newer ventures.

For instance, renewable energy company Zelestra has secured over $557M in funding. Other companies like Multiverse Computing are tackling complex problems in the quantum software field.

Below is our list of 100 Spain startups worth keeping an eye on.
