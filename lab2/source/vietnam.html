Top 100 Vietnam Startups to Watch in 2026

Startups

Vietnam's startup ecosystem shows significant growth across diverse sectors, from retail to financial technology. The country's companies are attracting considerable investment from both local and international backers.

E-commerce and payments company VNLIFE leads with $550M in total funding. Other notable companies include electric motorbike maker Dat Bike, which has secured $41.9M.

Below is our list of 100 Vietnam startups worth keeping an eye on.
