Top 61 Taiwan Startups to Watch in 2026

Startups

Taiwan's startup ecosystem is a mix of established hardware companies and newer software ventures. The region shows strength in areas from manufacturing to artificial intelligence.

For example, ProLogium Technology has raised $537.5M for its solid state battery development. Cybersecurity firm TXOne Networks has also secured significant funding with $144.9M.

Below is our list of 61 Taiwan startups worth keeping an eye on.
