Top 100 Belgium Startups to Watch in 2026

Startups

Belgium's startup scene is a mix of established software companies and newer ventures in various fields. The country shows strong activity in sectors like SaaS, AI, and mobility.

For example, Odoo has secured over $1.1 billion in funding for its business software. Meanwhile, recent cybersecurity startup Aikido Security raised $24.6 million since its founding in 2022.

Below is our list of 100 Belgium startups worth keeping an eye on.

Cloud Security Cyber Security Penetration Testing Software

Aikido Security provides a unified platform for code and cloud security, built specifically for developers. Their tool consolidates alerts from various scanners, helping teams prioritize and fix critical vulnerabilities without disrupting their workflow.
