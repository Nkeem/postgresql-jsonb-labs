Top 41 Chile Startups to Watch in 2026

Startups

Chile's startup ecosystem shows a notable diversity across various industries. The landscape features both emerging companies and well funded ventures.

Energy company HIF Global leads in funding with $480M for its carbon neutral fuel production. Betterfly follows with $204.5M for its platform that converts healthy activities into charitable donations.

Below is our list of 41 Chile startups worth keeping an eye on.

Energy Energy Management Fuel

HIF Global produces carbon-neutral synthetic fuels, known as eFuels, designed to work in existing cars, ships, and planes without modification. Their process uses renewable energy to create a gasoline substitute, offering a way to decarbonize the transportation sector.
